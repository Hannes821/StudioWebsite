1.  Basic  Setup
This  process  is  necessary  to  bring  your  changes  to  the  main  branch.  We  split  in  branches  to
make

sure

conflicts

are

resolved

  You  have  changes  on  your  local  branch.  These  are  reviewed  by  the  manager  and  need  to  be

change

per

se

unwanted.

FF-OI-06: “Claim Administration” Rev01

Confidential Operation Instruction

Table of Contents
1. Gain Access
2. Discuss Claims Details
3. Submission of Work
4. Review & Completion





1. Gain Access
The Management Board is being stored at Trello:
https://trello.com/b/k4e2XoqY/ca-imp

provide your work email address.
1. Get added by the admin to the repository as collaborator;
2. Confirm that you can access it.
3. Make yourself familiar with the structure:
a. Personal “Work in progress” piles left, Milestone piles right
2. Discuss Details of a Claim
Taking over a claim (work package) is indicated by moving a card from any milestone pile to
your personal “wip” pile. Additionally, add your avatar /get your avatar added on the card, and
make sure it has the tags for the current (and following necessary) work stages.
Before starting hands -on, make s ure you d iscussed boundary conditions and details
sufficiently with the Trello admin (claim manager) and the artist/develo per of the follow -up
stages. This is to ensure that we have little to no “after work fixes ”. Attached links, f iles,
checklists might help you, also you are invited t o discuss details on discord with the team to
support you and gain maximum efficiency.

3. Submission of work
When you think you are done, send the files via discord or gdrive or git push to the admin.
Then, the card will be moved from your “Work in Progress” pile to the “Review” pile.

## Purpose and key points

Enter cheats in chat using `/`; `/testersrights` enables testing rights. Data lives in `CA_DT_Cheats`, with implementation in `CA_ServerCheatsComponent` on BP_RTSController. Row Name and Cheat field must match. Add `ExecuteCheat_*` functions and connect them through `ExecuteCheat`. Use public interfaces.

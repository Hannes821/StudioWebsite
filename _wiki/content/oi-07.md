## Purpose and key points

Covers historical maps, regional biomes and the pipeline from geographic data through CloudCompare and DCC/heightmaps into Unreal. Includes automaterial, boundaries, water, navmesh, weather, animals and camps. Some methods are experimental or incomplete. **Version note:** UE4 references and a UE-5.5+ Nav Link note must not be transferred to UE 5.2 without checking.

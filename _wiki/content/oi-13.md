## Purpose and key points

Frame colours communicate roles: red military; orange siege/construction; blue agents/Church; light green civilians/domestic animals; dark green wildlife. The outer ring is the main class, the inner ring the subclass; lords use gold accents. **These are not player colours.** Buildings, Resources and Actions are currently headings only.

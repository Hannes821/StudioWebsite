## Purpose and key points

IndividualTag connects the Blueprint to `DT_Objects_info`. Construction and Destruction mesh arrays need at least one entry and follow Health from 0 to 100%. Spline buildings require PillarsMeshes. Costs use RequiredResources; splines also use CostUnitDistance. Building storage is documented as a PlayerState storage proxy. Add ResourceProduction and WorkersComponent only when needed.

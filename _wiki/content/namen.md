## Character backgrounds

`LordClasses` includes Utlagatus, Mercator, Dominus, Venator, Clericus, Eques, Scolasticus, Generalis, Medicus, Mechanicus and Faber Ferrarius.

`NameF` and `NameM` contain regional personal-name lists. `NamingConvention` concerns technical asset names. These are three separate naming systems.

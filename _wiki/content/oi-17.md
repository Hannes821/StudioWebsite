## Purpose and key points

Abstract `BaseAttributeComponent` encapsulates Value, MaxValue, initialisation and optional regeneration. Health, Stability, Experience, Morale and Stamina build on it. `UpdateValue` resets the regeneration timer; `SetValue` broadcasts a change; `SetValueSilent` does not. Zero Health means death; Stability can recover from zero. Morale returns to a baseline. Stamina regenerates between StartRest and StopRest. See [Technical architecture](technik.html) for details and dispatchers.

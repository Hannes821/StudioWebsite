## Purpose and key points

`BP_Submenu_Buildings` contains Page Manager, MenuPage and index-aligned Pages. The documented limit is ten buttons per submenu. A missing Class in `DT_Objects_info` prevents spawn/cost resolution. A red devil is the missing-icon placeholder. The example uses `Building.Category.Communal.Granary`.

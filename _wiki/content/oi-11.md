## Purpose and key points

Damage type `CA_DamageType` is separate from delivery `E_CA_DamageDeliveryType`. Standard attacks use server-side ApplyDamage and HealthComponent; Delivery/Piercing come through `BPI_DamageDealer`. Effect Damage follows a separate path without Evade/Block. **Unresolved:** pages 6/12 describe Piercing as a damage portion bypassing block; page 7 describes reduced block chance. Kinetic impact, Attack speed and Anim notify remain under development.
